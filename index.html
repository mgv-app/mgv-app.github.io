Milner ProjectApp

Put this on your home screen once. After that, tap the icon and it opens straight into the app.

Open the app
iPhone (Chrome, Safari or Edge)
  1. Tap the share icon (in Chrome it's in the address bar; in Edge, tap ⋯ first).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  4. Tap Add.
Android (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Tap the ⋯ button at the bottom.
  2. Tap Add to phone (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Add, then Add again if asked.

The first time you open it from the icon, sign in with your work email and PIN.
